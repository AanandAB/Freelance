Onapookkal · ഓണപ്പൂക്കൾ

Live · onapookkal.store

A seasonal Onam flower e-commerce site for a Kannur shop — bilingual (English + മലയാളം), selling pookalam flowers, petal packs and daily pookalam kits with a live countdown to Thiruvonam. Distance-based delivery, flexible payments and a full admin CRM with profit-and-loss and a Leaflet delivery map.

  • Admin CRM — products, orders, coupons, delivery map and profit-and-loss
