// Full-Stack Web App + CMS

Happy Aquarium

Live · Kuthuparamba, Kerala

Premium storefront and a complete custom CMS for an aquarium and imported-fish store. A "nature-documentary" editorial design with a searchable live catalogue of 35+ fish species, rich care-attribute detail pages, and interactive tools. The /admin backend lets the owner run everything — stock, offers, enquiries and the homepage — from a browser, on a stack that costs nothing to host.

ResultRuns on Cloudflare Workers + D1 (OpenNext) — a real store live at ₹0/month hosting.
What's inside
  • Live catalogue of 35+ fish species — search, filters and sorting
  • Interactive Fish Compatibility Checker and Aquarium Planner
  • Custom /admin CMS — signed-cookie auth, role hierarchy, 30+ field fish CRUD
  • Live-stats dashboard, low-stock alerts and enquiries inbox
  • Reorderable homepage builder and scheduled offers
  • WhatsApp reserve/call CTAs and live-countdown offers
